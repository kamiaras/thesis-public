\subsection*{Disclosure of AI Assistance}
\label{sec:ai-assistance}

I used ChatGPT, including GPT-5.6 and GPT-6, in preparing this dissertation.
The account below describes the scope of this assistance by chapter.
Brief chapter-opening notes also identify AI-generated material and
specific mathematical contributions.

\noindent\textbf{Abstract and Chapters~\ref{Section:Introduction}
and~\ref{Section:Conclusion}.}
AI provided substantial assistance in rewriting and reorganizing my initial
drafts of the abstract, introduction, and conclusion.

\noindent\textbf{Chapter~\ref{Section:Preliminaries}.}
This chapter was generated primarily using AI. The assistance included
rewriting and reorganizing my initial drafts, selecting relevant material
from other chapters, and bringing it together into a coherent presentation
of the mathematical background.

\noindent\textbf{Chapter~\ref{Section:projfree-domain}.}
I used ChatGPT, primarily GPT-6 with some use of GPT-5.6, to refine proofs
and improve the chapter's organization and editorial presentation.

\noindent\textbf{Chapter~\ref{Section:projfree}.}
GPT-6 Astra suggested replacing the constraints by their positive parts
within the original update rules, developed the corresponding proofs, and
helped with the chapter's exposition. ChatGPT also helped me rewrite and
shorten the proof, improve the parameter choices, and reduce the constants.

\par\Needspace{6\baselineskip}
\noindent\textbf{Chapter~\ref{Section:ChapEnergyharvest}.}
Most of the changes made to the published work for this chapter were
produced with AI assistance at my request. These included reorganizing
the exposition and proofs, making the conditional-mean assumption
explicit, correcting and completing the sufficient battery-capacity
calculation, tightening the term involving the initial potential in the
regret bound, and making step-size tuning explicit.

\noindent\textbf{Chapter~\ref{Section:ChapControl}.}
I used ChatGPT to refine and reorganize my proofs and improve their
presentation, constants, and arguments. The lower-bound construction and
proofs in Section~\ref{ch4:sec:regret-lower-bound} were developed primarily
using ChatGPT (5.6 sol), as disclosed in the source manuscript.

\noindent\textbf{Chapter~\ref{Section:ChapRateAdaptation}.}
My use of AI in preparing the original joint paper was limited to
editorial assistance. For the dissertation, AI helped improve constants,
refine proof steps, and improve the presentation of the analysis for a
known lower bound on service slack. The statement and proof of
Theorem~\ref{ch5:upper:thm:ucb-log-lower}, which establishes the necessity
of the logarithmic factor for the specific UCB1 algorithm used here, were
generated by AI for this dissertation and are not part of the original paper.

\noindent\textbf{Formatting and submission support.}
ChatGPT also assisted with the dissertation's front matter, including
this Preface, as well as formatting, bibliography checks, and preparation
for submission.
