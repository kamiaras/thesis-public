\subsection*{Disclosure of AI Assistance}

I used ChatGPT, including GPT-5.6 and GPT-6, in preparing this dissertation.
The scope of this assistance is described below.

\noindent\textbf{Abstract and Chapters~\ref{Section:Introduction},
\ref{Section:Preliminaries}, and~\ref{Section:Conclusion}.}
AI provided substantial assistance in rewriting and reorganizing my initial
drafts of the abstract, introduction (Chapter~\ref{Section:Introduction}),
mathematical preliminaries (Chapter~\ref{Section:Preliminaries}), and
conclusion (Chapter~\ref{Section:Conclusion}). For
Chapter~\ref{Section:Preliminaries}, this assistance also included selecting
relevant material from other chapters and bringing it together into a
coherent presentation of the mathematical background.

\noindent\textbf{Chapter~\ref{Section:projfree-domain}.}
I used ChatGPT to refine the
proofs and improve the chapter's organization and exposition.

\noindent\textbf{Chapter~\ref{Section:projfree}.}
ChatGPT suggested replacing the full constraint function with its clipped
version within the same update rule. It also helped me rewrite and shorten
the proof, improve the parameter choices, and reduce the constant in the
joint bound on objective gap and squared constraint violation.

\noindent\textbf{Chapter~\ref{Section:ChapEnergyharvest}.}
AI helped me improve the exposition and refine the proofs.

\noindent\textbf{Chapter~\ref{Section:ChapControl}.}
I used ChatGPT to refine and reorganize my proofs, improve their
presentation, and sharpen the constants. The lower-bound construction
and proofs in Section~\ref{ch4:sec:regret-lower-bound} were developed
primarily using ChatGPT.

\noindent\textbf{Chapter~\ref{Section:ChapRateAdaptation}.}
My use of AI in preparing the original joint paper was limited to
editorial assistance. For the dissertation, AI helped me improve the
constants and refine proof steps in my analysis. The statement and proof
of the UCB1-specific logarithmic lower bound were generated by AI.

\noindent\textbf{Formatting and submission support.}
ChatGPT also assisted with the dissertation's formatting, bibliography
checks, and preparation for submission.
